# 06 · Terms of Use — Sagara Dhwani / Taluk Channel Network

**Version:** 0.1 (draft for legal review) · **Operator:** [Entity name — open item §12.5]

> Working standard to be confirmed by a media lawyer before launch (plan §12.1).

## 1. What this covers
The Sagara Dhwani channel page, our content on third-party platforms (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp), the viewer tip line, and our advertising services.

## 2. Who we are and what we are not
A digital-only local channel whose purpose is to attach people to their own place — its history, personalities, culture, livelihood, sports, youth and literature — with daily taluk, district, state and central current affairs. We are not a television broadcaster and not a news agency.

## 3. Our content
- **Our footage, scripts, narration, graphics and AI-made visuals are ours.** You may share links and use platform share features. You may not re-upload our videos as your own, crop our logo, or use our content commercially without written permission.
- **Narration is an AI Kannada voice** used as the channel's identity. Interviews and on-camera people are real human voices. AI-generated visuals are used only in history, culture, identity pieces and advertisements — never in news. Where AI visuals appear, the piece is labelled.
- **Retold stories.** Where we retell facts first reported elsewhere, we write our own Kannada script from the facts and credit the original source where appropriate. Facts are not owned; expression is.
- **Corrections.** We correct errors on every platform where the item appeared.

## 4. Viewer contributions and the tip line
- By sending us video, photos, audio or text you confirm you made it (or have the right to share it), that everyone identifiable in it knows it may be published, and that any minor in it has a parent's consent.
- We publish a submission only with your **written permission in the chat**, which we save. You keep ownership; you grant us a non-exclusive, perpetual licence to use, edit and archive it with credit.
- We verify before publishing and may decline without reason. Unverified viral clips are not presented as fact.
- Do not send CCTV or accident footage of identifiable victims, private footage of anyone without their consent, or anything you do not have the right to share.

## 5. Local creators (roster)
If you give us standing permission to use clips from your public posts, that one consent covers future use with your name and handle on screen. You may withdraw it at any time by message; we stop further use within 7 days.

## 6. Comments and participation
Participation features (question of the week, citizen voices) live on the platforms. Platform rules apply. We may feature a comment or question in a piece with attribution to the public handle.

## 7. Advertising
- Advertising is sold **only by our central sales desk on a fixed, published rate card**. Taluk staff cannot offer discounts, promise coverage or collect money. If anyone claims otherwise, tell us.
- **Paid content is always labelled** "Paid" / "Sponsored". A dedicated story about an advertiser is marked as such. **No coverage is ever promised or given in exchange for advertising.**
- Prohibited categories are listed in the [Advertising Policy](08-advertising-policy.md) and attached to the rate card.
- **Political advertising** is sold and certified centrally only, pre-certified by the Media Certification and Monitoring Committee where required, labelled with who paid, and not run in the 48-hour silence period before polling. Paid news is never accepted.
- Payments are by bank transfer, UPI or cheque against a GST invoice. We do not accept cash.

## 8. Rights complaints and takedowns
If you believe our content infringes your rights or publishes your personal data without consent, write to editor@[domain]. The item comes down immediately while we examine it. We will respond within 7 days.

## 9. Acceptable use of the channel page
No scraping for commercial reuse, no attempts to access the operations system, no impersonation of the channel or its staff.

## 10. Liability
Content is provided for information and cultural purposes. We take care to verify, but we do not guarantee completeness. To the extent permitted by Indian law, we are not liable for indirect losses arising from use of the content. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.

## 11. Governing law
India; courts at [Shimoga / Bengaluru], Karnataka.
