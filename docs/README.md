## Status legend used across documents

- **Decided** — settled in the plan (17.1).
- **Open** — unresolved (17.2 / 17.3); the document records positions, not a decision.
- **Assumption** — working figure, not researched; must be validated.

## Hard rules that every document inherits

1. Every taluk publishes every single day.
2. No AI visuals in news, ever. AI visuals only for history, culture, identity and ads.
3. No borrowed footage as content. Green/amber/red rules; amber needs central clearance.
4. Political advertising sold, certified and labelled centrally only. No paid news, ever.
5. Fixed central rate card. Taluk staff cannot discount, promise coverage or collect cash.
6. Every rupee invoiced and banked.
7. Publishing happens from the state desk only.

> These documents are working standards, not legal, tax or financial advice. A CA and a media lawyer confirm sections 05–09 before launch (plan 12.1).
