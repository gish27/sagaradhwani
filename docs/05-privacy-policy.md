# 05 · Privacy Policy — Sagara Dhwani / Taluk Channel Network

**Effective:** [date of launch] · **Version:** 0.1 (draft for legal review) · **Controller:** [Entity name — open item §12.5], Karnataka, India · **Contact:** privacy@[domain]

> Drafted to align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP) and platform policies. Must be reviewed by a media lawyer before publication (plan §12.1). Working standard, not legal advice.

## 1. Who we are

Sagara Dhwani is a digital-only local channel for Sagara taluk, part of the Taluk Channel Network. We publish daily video, audio and posters on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp, and on our own channel page. We are not a television broadcaster.

## 2. What personal data we collect and why

| Category | Examples | Purpose | Legal basis (DPDP) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **People who appear in our content** | Name, designation, place, voice, likeness, opinions | Journalism and identity content about the taluk | Consent for interviews and submissions; journalistic/public-interest reporting for public events and public officials acting in their role |
| **Viewer submissions and tips** | Phone number, WhatsApp name, submitted video/photos/text, location | Verify and, with permission, publish | Consent (written in chat, saved) |
| **Local creators on our roster** | Name, handle, platform, standing permission | Credit and reuse of public clips | Consent (standing permission) |
| **Advertisers** | Business name, contact, phone, GSTIN, bank reference, invoices | Contracts, invoicing, GST compliance, collections | Contract; legal obligation |
| **Staff and contractors** | Name, phone, role, declared affiliations, personal-page terms, on-call | Employment/contract, editorial accountability | Contract; legitimate use |
| **Channel page visitors** | Page views, approximate region, device type (aggregated) | Understand audience; agency reporting | Legitimate use; no cross-site tracking |
| **Children** | Likeness only with a parent's consent; never otherwise | Identity/culture pieces (school events, sports) | Verifiable parental consent |

We do **not** collect payment card data (payments are by bank transfer, UPI or cheque — never cash), biometric data, or precise location.

## 3. Sources

Directly from you (interviews, submissions, contracts); from public events we film; from official government feeds (PIB, department releases, legislature proceedings); from local creators under standing permission; from the platforms we publish on (aggregate analytics only).

## 4. How we use data

- Produce, verify and publish daily content and identity pieces.
- Keep an archive of identity pieces (see §8 Retention).
- Credit contributors on screen and in descriptions.
- Run advertising contracts, issue GST invoices, follow up on collections.
- Comply with election law (disclosure of who paid for political advertising; MCMC certification records).
- Respond to rights complaints, corrections and takedown requests.
- Report aggregated audience numbers to advertisers and agencies (never individual viewers).

We do not sell personal data. We do not use AI to generate visuals of real people in news content, ever.

## 5. Sharing

| With | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Publishing platforms (YouTube, Meta, WhatsApp) | Published content | Distribution; governed by their policies |
| Service providers (hosting, storage, Kannada text-to-speech, AI image generation) | Scripts, footage, backups | Processing on our instructions under contract |
| Chartered accountant, auditors | Advertiser and invoice data | Tax and compliance |
| Election authorities (MCMC/ECI) | Political advertising records | Legal requirement |
| Law enforcement / courts | As required by law | Legal obligation |

No cross-border transfer is intended for personal data of individuals in content; backups are stored in India where provider options allow.

## 6. Your rights (DPDP Act 2023)

You may: access a summary of your personal data we hold; correct or update it; request erasure where it is no longer needed for the purpose or where consent is withdrawn; nominate a person to exercise rights on your behalf; and raise a grievance. Write to privacy@[domain] or WhatsApp [number]. We respond within 30 days.

**Withdrawing consent for published content:** we will stop further use and remove the item from our own page and archive within 7 days. Content already published on platforms will be removed by us where we control it; copies made by others are outside our control. Journalistic content about public officials in their public role may be retained in the public interest; we will tell you if we rely on this.

## 7. Children

We never publish footage of a minor without a parent's or guardian's consent recorded in writing. If you believe we have, contact us and we will take the item down the same day while we examine it.

## 8. Retention

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Published identity pieces (history, culture, personalities, etc.) | Permanent archive — these are the record of the taluk. Subject to erasure requests above |
| Published daily news | Retained on platforms; archived copies 3 years |
| Raw footage not used | 90 days, then deleted |
| Viewer submissions not published | 30 days |
| Consent records | For as long as the related content is retained + 3 years |
| Advertiser contracts, invoices, payments | 8 years (tax) |
| Political advertising certification records | 8 years or as required by ECI |
| Staff records | Duration of engagement + 3 years |
| Analytics (aggregate) | Indefinitely, non-personal |

## 9. Security

Role-based access; publishing from the central desk only; consent references attached to every piece; audit trail of changes; encrypted backups; least-privilege access to raw footage. Staff sign the shared rules (plan §8.2) including dignity and consent rules.

## 10. Corrections, complaints and takedowns

- Factual corrections: write to editor@[domain]; we correct on all platforms and note the correction.
- Rights complaints: the item comes down immediately while we examine it (plan §6.6).
- Grievance Officer: [name], [contact], as required under Indian IT rules.
